## Transferable lesson
- **cue**: When generating persuasive, policy-driven content for a senior leadership audience, especially when translating raw data (survey results, metrics) into mandatory structural or policy changes.
- **lesson**: When defining the agent's structural constraints, always enforce a mandatory, multi-stage narrative arc (e.g., Problem $\rightarrow$ Evidence $\rightarrow$ Implication $\rightarrow$ Solution $\rightarrow$ CTA) in both the `guardrails.md` and the `systemprompt.md`. Furthermore, augment the `LongTermMEMORY.md` with domain-specific knowledge that explicitly teaches the agent to synthesize data into a "So what?" moment (Implication/Synthesis) and frame the findings in terms of measurable business risk (e.g., "retention risk," "operational efficiency").
- **evidence**: The successful candidates (agg1, dom1, dom2, inv2) all implemented the mandatory Policy Arc structure in `guardrails.md` and `systemprompt.md`, and integrated the synthesis requirement in `LongTermMEMORY.md`. This combination forced the agent to move beyond simple data reporting (seen in base1/base2) to generating high-stakes, actionable policy mandates, as demonstrated by the consistent 6-slide structure and the mandatory Implication slide across the successful deliverables.
- **confidence**: high, because the combination of structural constraints (guardrails/systemprompt) and domain knowledge (LongTermMEMORY) was required to achieve the single most critical feature of the output: the mandatory, persuasive policy mandate.
